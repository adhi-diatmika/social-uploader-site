Vidantara

Vidantara is an Indonesian educational channel that turns current news into short explainer videos about how things work: public budgets, rules, rights, and the systems behind the headlines.

Vidantara Uploader

Vidantara Uploader is a private tool used only by the Vidantara team to publish Vidantara's own videos. It uploads finished videos to the channel's own accounts on YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok. On TikTok, videos are sent to the creator's inbox as drafts, then reviewed and posted manually in the TikTok app.

The tool is not offered to the public and does not access any account other than Vidantara's own.
