Social Uploader

Social Uploader is a private publishing tool. Its owner uses it to publish short educational videos from their own channels to the channels' own accounts on YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok.

On TikTok, videos are sent to the creator's inbox as drafts, then reviewed and posted manually in the TikTok app.

The tool is not offered to the public and only accesses accounts that belong to its owner.
